About ESRAA GALLERY

ESRAA GALLERY is a handmade boutique brand creating elegant resin art, candles, pottery, accessories, custom gifts, and home decor.

Every piece is crafted with care and designed to bring beauty, warmth, and meaning into everyday spaces and special moments.

Based in New Jersey, ESRAA GALLERY celebrates handmade details, feminine design, thoughtful gifting, and one-of-a-kind boutique quality.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about handmade pieces, custom orders, payment, shipping, and product care.

Are ESRAA GALLERY products handmade?

Yes. ESRAA GALLERY products are handmade with care. Each piece may have small unique details that make it one of a kind.

Can I request a custom handmade order?

Yes. You can request custom colors, themes, names, florals, gift sets, or special occasion pieces through the Custom Orders or Checkout section.

How do I pay for my order?

You can choose Zelle/manual bank payment, Venmo Business payment link, Stripe payment link, or PayPal payment link. This website does not collect card numbers directly.

Do handmade pieces look exactly like the photos?

Not always. Handmade items may vary slightly in color, flower placement, resin movement, finish, or texture. These variations make each piece unique.

How long does processing take?

Processing time depends on the item, quantity, customization, and season. Add your deadline in the order note so ESRAA GALLERY can confirm availability.

Can I order gifts for special occasions?

Yes. ESRAA GALLERY handmade pieces are perfect for birthdays, holidays, weddings, home decor, thank-you gifts, and meaningful custom gifting.
