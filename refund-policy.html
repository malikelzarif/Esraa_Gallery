Exchange & Refund Policy

Handmade order policy

ESRAA GALLERY creates handmade pieces with care. Because each item is made by hand, customers should review the policy below before placing an order or submitting a custom request.

Handmade Item Notice

Each handmade item may include small natural variations in color, finish, flowers, resin movement, shape, texture, or pattern. These details are part of the handmade character and uniqueness of each piece.

Custom Orders

Custom handmade orders are created specifically for the customer. Once production begins, custom orders may not be eligible for cancellation, exchange, or refund.

Order Review Before Production

For custom products, customers should confirm colors, names, initials, florals, themes, quantity, deadlines, and pricing before production begins.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact ESRAA GALLERY with clear photos, order details, and a description of the issue as soon as possible so the order can be reviewed.

Returns & Exchanges

Return or exchange eligibility may depend on the product type, condition, customization, and timing. Handmade and custom products may have limited return eligibility.

Final Sale Items

Sale items, personalized products, custom-made items, and limited handmade pieces may be considered final sale unless ESRAA GALLERY confirms otherwise.

Payment & Order Confirmation

Payment is completed through external payment options such as Zelle, Venmo Business, Stripe link, or PayPal link. The website does not collect or store credit card numbers.

Final Confirmation

Final exchange, refund, shipping, pickup, and custom order terms should be confirmed directly with ESRAA GALLERY before payment or order fulfillment.

Custom Order Policy

Personalized pieces need clear approval

Custom handmade orders require careful confirmation before production. This protects both the customer and the maker by setting clear expectations.

  • Confirm colors, flowers, initials, names, themes, and quantity.
  • Confirm deadline, shipping, pickup, and payment details.
  • Understand that handmade pieces may not be identical every time.
  • Review final custom order terms before production begins.

Policy Questions

Common policy questions

These quick answers help customers understand the exchange and refund policy before ordering.

Can I return a custom handmade order?

Custom handmade orders may not be eligible for return or cancellation once production begins because they are made specifically for the customer.

What if my product looks slightly different from the photo?

Handmade products may include slight natural variations in color, flowers, resin movement, texture, or finish. These details make each item unique.

What should I do if my item arrives damaged?

Contact ESRAA GALLERY with clear photos, your order details, and a description of the issue so the order can be reviewed.

Are sale items final sale?

Sale items, personalized pieces, and limited handmade items may be final sale unless ESRAA GALLERY confirms otherwise.
