What to include

Product type, colors, names, initials, flowers, theme, quantity, deadline, pickup or shipping preference, and inspiration details.

  • Example: “Custom resin bookmark with pink flowers and gold flakes.”
  • Example: “Custom chess set in emerald, white, and gold.”
  • Example: “Gift box for birthday with candle and resin accessory.”
  • Example: “Home decor tray with cream, green, and warm gold details.”

Custom Order Notes

Important before ordering

Handmade variations

Custom handmade pieces may include slight natural variations in color, finish, flowers, resin movement, shape, texture, or pattern.

Approval before production

Colors, names, initials, florals, themes, quantity, deadline, and pricing should be confirmed before production begins.

Custom order cancellation

Custom orders may not be eligible for cancellation, exchange, or refund once production begins because they are made specifically for the customer.

Payment confirmation

Payment is completed through external payment options. This website does not collect or store credit card numbers.
