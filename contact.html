Quick Answers

Before you contact us

These common answers can help customers understand handmade orders before sending a message.

Can I request a custom handmade product?

Yes. You can request custom colors, themes, names, florals, initials, gift sets, and special occasion pieces.

How do I pay for my order?

You can choose Zelle/manual payment, Venmo Business payment link, Stripe payment link, or PayPal payment link.

Can I ask about a product before ordering?

Yes. Use the contact form or social links to ask about size, color, material, availability, custom options, or care notes.

Do handmade products have slight variations?

Yes. Handmade pieces may vary slightly in color, flowers, resin movement, shape, finish, or texture. These variations make each item unique.
