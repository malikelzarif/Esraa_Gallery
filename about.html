About ESRAA GALLERY

ESRAA GALLERY is a handmade boutique brand creating elegant resin art, candles, pottery, accessories, custom gifts, and home decor.

Every piece is crafted with care and designed to bring beauty, warmth, and meaning into everyday spaces and special moments.

Based in New Jersey, ESRAA GALLERY celebrates handmade details, feminine design, thoughtful gifting, and one-of-a-kind boutique quality.
