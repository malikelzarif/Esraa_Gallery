Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about handmade products, custom orders, payment, shipping, care, and ESRAA GALLERY policies.

Are ESRAA GALLERY products handmade?

Yes. ESRAA GALLERY products are handmade with care. Each piece may include small natural variations that make it one of a kind.

Can I request a custom handmade order?

Yes. You can request custom colors, themes, names, initials, florals, gift sets, or special occasion pieces through the Custom Orders page or Checkout / Order Request form.

What products does ESRAA GALLERY offer?

ESRAA GALLERY offers handmade resin art, resin dominoes, bookmarks, resin hair combs, custom resin chess sets, tic-tac-toe pieces, candles, pottery, ceramic bowls, accessories, bags, home decor, and custom gifts.

How do I pay for my order?

Customers can choose Zelle/manual bank payment, Venmo Business payment link, optional Stripe payment link, or optional PayPal payment link.

Does this website collect credit card information?

No. This website does not collect or store credit card numbers. Card payments should be completed only through external payment links such as Stripe or PayPal.

Why does checkout say “order request”?

This website is built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript only. The checkout form creates an order request, saves the order summary in localStorage, and shows payment instructions. A real email service or backend can be connected later.

Do handmade products look exactly like the photos?

Not always. Handmade pieces may vary slightly in color, flowers, resin movement, texture, shape, or finish. These variations are normal and make each item unique.

Can I choose custom colors?

Yes. Many custom products can be made with requested colors, themes, gold details, florals, names, initials, or occasion-based design ideas.

How long does a custom order take?

Processing time depends on the product type, design complexity, quantity, availability, and season. Add your deadline in the order note so ESRAA GALLERY can confirm timing.

Can I order gifts for special occasions?

Yes. ESRAA GALLERY handmade pieces are perfect for birthdays, weddings, holidays, thank-you gifts, housewarming gifts, home decor, and meaningful custom gifting.

What should I include in a custom order request?

Include the product type, colors, names, initials, flowers, theme, quantity, event date, shipping or pickup preference, and any inspiration details.

Are custom orders refundable?

Custom handmade orders may not be eligible for cancellation, exchange, or refund once production begins because they are made specifically for the customer.

What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact ESRAA GALLERY with clear photos, order details, and a description of the issue as soon as possible so the order can be reviewed.

Can I add a gift note?

Yes. Add your gift message, color request, or special instructions in the cart note or checkout order note field.

How do I care for resin products?

Keep resin pieces away from extreme heat, harsh chemicals, direct prolonged sunlight, and rough handling. Clean gently with a soft cloth.
